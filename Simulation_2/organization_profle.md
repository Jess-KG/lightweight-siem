# Organisation Profile

**Organisation:** Southern Cross Financial Services (SCFS)
**Industry:** Financial Services
**Location:** Melbourne, Australia
**Employees:** 250 (simulated environment: 9 named accounts + 2 service accounts, across 6 systems)

## Critical Information

- Customer account and transaction records
- Employee personal and payroll information
- Authentication credentials (local + domain)
- Internal finance application data

## Critical Systems

- Active Directory (domain: `SCFS.LOCAL`)
- File server (shared drives: Finance, HR, IT)
- Finance application server
- Employee workstations (Finance, HR, IT)

## Security Objectives

- Protect customer and financial data from unauthorised access
- Detect and respond to credential misuse or compromise
- Maintain integrity and availability of core systems
- Preserve a reliable audit trail of authentication and system activity

## Operating Hours

Standard business hours: **Monday–Friday, 08:00–18:00 AEST**. A small IT
team (1 person) occasionally works outside these hours for maintenance.
No other staff have a routine business reason to be active outside this
window.
